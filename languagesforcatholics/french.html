Foi. Culture. Élan.

Live online French classes for Catholic children, rooted in faith and Francophone culture.

Our Levels

Éveil

Ages 3–7

An awakening to French through songs, stories, and simple prayers. Children absorb the sounds and rhythms of the language in a gentle, playful environment. No reading or writing required.

Élémentaire

Ages 7–10

Building vocabulary and basic structures through conversation, stories, and cultural content. Children begin to read and write in French, learning about saints, French history, and Catholic life in the Francophone world.

Grammaire

Ages 10–18

Systematic grammar instruction alongside literature, history, and more advanced conversation. Students work towards genuine fluency and a deep understanding of French as a living language with a Catholic soul.

How It Works

  • Live classes via Zoom, small groups (typically 4–8 students)
  • Weekly sessions during UK term time
  • Homework and practice materials provided
  • Regular progress updates for parents
