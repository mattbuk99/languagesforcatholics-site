Live online Italian classes for Catholic children, rooted in faith and Italian culture.

Our Levels

Nido

Ages 3–7

A gentle introduction to Italian through songs, stories, and prayer. Children begin to hear and speak the language of Dante, of Rome, of the Church's heart in Italy.

Elementare

Ages 7–10

Building vocabulary and basic structures through conversation, stories, and cultural content.

Grammatica

Ages 10–18

Systematic grammar instruction alongside literature, history, and more advanced conversation.

We are building our Italian programme thoughtfully. If you're interested in future levels, please get in touch and we'll keep you informed as classes become available.
