Faith. Culture. Fluency.

We teach French, Italian, Spanish and English as a foreign language to Catholic children and adults, through live online classes rooted in the faith and culture of the language.

Our tutors are practising Catholics and native or near-native speakers. Our curriculum draws on the saints, the liturgy, and the rich heritage of Catholic Europe. Students learn vocabulary and grammar, but more than that, they encounter the living cultures that shaped Christendom.

We offer small group tuition online for ages 3 to 18, and for adults of all ages, from first words to formal grammar. Families across the UK and USA join us each week.

Why families choose us

Tutors who share your faith

Every tutor is a practising Catholic. The faith isn't an add-on; it's woven through how and what we teach.

Cultural depth, not just tourist phrases

We teach language as a gateway to civilisation. Students go beyond "la gare" and "il ristorante" to meet Sainte Thérèse and Dante.

Live, small group tuition

Real interaction with a real teacher. Our classes are capped to ensure every child is known, heard, and stretched.

Flexible for all families

Most of our families homeschool, but we welcome anyone seeking rigorous, faith-consistent language education.
