Fe. Cultura. Pasión.

Live online Spanish classes for Catholic children, rooted in faith and Spanish culture.

Our Levels

Despertar

Ages 3–7

A gentle introduction to Spanish through songs, stories, and prayer. Children begin to hear and speak the language of Cervantes, of St Ignatius, of the Church's heart in Spain.

Elemental

Ages 7–10

Building vocabulary and basic structures through conversation, stories, and cultural content.

Gramática

Ages 10–18

Systematic grammar instruction alongside literature, history, and more advanced conversation.

Despertar and Elemental begin in September and are open for booking now. Gramática will follow as the programme grows. If that is the level your child needs, do get in touch and we will keep you informed.
